BlockRazor is a leading Web3 transaction infrastructure platform built for wallets, DEXs, trading bots, searchers, and quantitative systems. It provides multi-chain transaction submission (RPC, Fast, Gas Sponsor, Bundle), high-performance data streams (Mempool, Block Stream, Node Stream, Network Fee), and a BSC Block Builder with a historical block production rate of 37% — the highest on the BNB Smart Chain.

## Get Started in 3 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Register at [blockrazor.io](https://blockrazor.io) and activate your account via the confirmation email. Copy your auth token from your account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a service">
    Pick the transaction submission mode or data stream that fits your workflow. New registered users get free access to RPC, Fast, Bundle, and Block Builder on Solana, BSC, Ethereum, and Base.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first request">
    Pass your auth token in the request header and submit your first transaction or open your first stream connection. See the [API Reference](/api-reference/rpc/bsc-json-rpc) for full endpoint documentation.
  </Step>
</Steps>
