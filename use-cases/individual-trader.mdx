You don't need to run a trading firm or a search operation to benefit from professional transaction infrastructure. Individual traders on BlockRazor get access to the same MEV-protected submission paths, global acceleration network, and atomic bundle execution that institutional users rely on — at no cost from the moment they register. Whether you're swapping tokens, minting NFTs, or executing a time-sensitive trade, BlockRazor's free tier gives you the tools to compete on equal footing.

## Core benefits for individual traders

<Steps>
  <Step title="MEV protection — stop leaving money on the table">
    When you submit a transaction through a standard public RPC, it sits in the public mempool where searchers can see it and front-run or sandwich it before it confirms. BlockRazor's RPC mode keeps your transaction private until block inclusion, preventing this class of value extraction entirely. For high-value swaps and time-sensitive trades, this protection can be worth more than the cost of any subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Faster confirmations with the global acceleration network">
    Fast mode connects to BlockRazor's distributed global relay and routes your transaction directly to highly staked validators. On Solana you get up to 3 TPS by default; on BSC and Base up to 10 TPS. For new registered users who want Fast mode without including a tip in the transaction, `SendTx` is available at 10 transactions per 5 seconds and 10 transactions per day — enough for active individual trading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gas Sponsor — trade before you hold gas">
    Gas Sponsor lets a platform or dApp cover your gas fee, meaning you can execute your first transaction on a new chain without needing to bridge native tokens first. As an individual trader, look for dApps and wallets that have integrated BlockRazor Gas Sponsor to enjoy this benefit automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade only when you need to">
    The free tier is designed to cover the needs of most individual traders indefinitely. If your volume or strategy complexity grows, BlockRazor's subscription services scale with you — from individual service add-ons to multi-chain packages — with discounts of up to 20% for longer commitment periods.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Register at [blockrazor.io](https://blockrazor.io). Your API key is generated immediately and grants access to all four free-tier submission modes across Solana, BSC, Ethereum, and Base.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your wallet or tool">
    Point your wallet's custom RPC endpoint to the BlockRazor protected RPC URL for your chosen chain. For programmatic use, replace the endpoint in your existing script — no other code changes are needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your first transaction">
    Send a transaction as normal. BlockRazor handles MEV protection, routing, and acceleration transparently. You can monitor submission status and transaction tracing from the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scale when ready">
    When your needs outgrow the free tier, browse individual service subscriptions or bundle packages from the pricing page. Discounts of 5–20% apply for 3–12 month commitments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start with the RPC endpoint as a simple drop-in replacement for your current provider. You get immediate MEV protection with zero configuration overhead, and you can layer in Fast mode or Bundle later as your trading strategy evolves.
</Tip>
